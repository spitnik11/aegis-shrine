# Art direction

- Cute fantasy pixel art with readable chibi silhouettes
- Original designs at a Pokémon-era gameplay scale, without copying Pokémon
- Fixed 2:1 isometric projection and 64x32 terrain footprint
- Upper-left lighting and consistent shadows
- Limited shared palette, hard pixel edges, nearest filtering
- No anti-aliasing, painterly gradients, photorealism, or perspective drift
